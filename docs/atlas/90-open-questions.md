| OPEN-40 | A topic's direction (the Explore badge, and the answer key's measured direction that both report versions are scored against) is its momentum within the range: New if first seen inside the range; otherwise the range's second half against its first half, Rising or Fading past a quarter either way, Steady in between. A burst early in the range that dies away reads Fading. Both report tasks define direction the same way. This replaces comparing the range's total with the period before, which called a dying burst Rising. | 2026-10-19 |
| OPEN-39 | Version A of the report, the one without retrieval, gets read access to posts but no retrieval by meaning: it may read posts written in the days it can see, a few at a time, in a fixed shuffle that has nothing to do with what the posts are about, and at most 10 posts per step (writing the report, then predicting the range), so it can't go round in a loop of turns. Version B keeps searching by meaning. Before this, version A had no tools at all and would not say anything concrete. On the same day Brian asked for both versions' report instructions to be nudged a little toward prediction: for each topic, where it looks to be heading next and why, with everything else left as it was.
